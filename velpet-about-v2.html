THE VELPET'S HISTORY

Velpet is a brand aiming to release new products for enhance the life of your pet.

The name Velpet combines the words "velvet" and "pet," giving it an elegant and warm meaning. Here is a possible interpretation for your brand. "Vel" comes from "velvet," which is associated with softness, luxury, and care. This evokes the idea of offering products that provide comfort and well-being to pets, emphasizing quality and affection. "Pet" refers directly to animals, making the name clear and easy to associate with the market.

Velpet as a brand

Velpet is a brand dedicated to improving the quality of life for pets, offering innovative and practical products that combine well-being, functionality, and design. We strive to create solutions that not only benefit pets but also make life easier for their owners. Committed to excellence, we provide high-quality products that promote the health, comfort, and well-being of your most loyal companions.

Velpet looks toward a future where care for pets goes hand in hand with care for the planet. We aim to create sustainable products using eco-friendly materials and recyclable packaging, while offering solutions adapted to pets of different breeds and sizes. Through personalized options, such as custom engraving, we seek to make every product more meaningful, functional, and uniquely made for each pet.

More than a brand

Velpet aims to build a meaningful connection that goes beyond our products. Through educational content about pet health, nutrition, and everyday care, we want to support owners throughout every stage of their pets’ lives. By creating a community where people can share experiences, advice, and their love for animals, Velpet seeks to become more than a pet brand—a space built around well-being, connection, and the bond we share with our companions.
